About

Chasing light
before the world wakes.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, I spend my early mornings and late evenings in the wild places that most people pass by on their way somewhere else — the ridgelines, the tidal flats, the old-growth corridors.

My work is about patience. About returning to the same spot across seasons until the light does something it has never done before. Nature photography is less about capturing a place than it is about bearing witness to a moment that will never repeat.

Available for editorial, fine art prints, and licensing. Inquiries welcome.
