About

The Bay in every
light and season.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, I spend my days — and often my nights — chasing the light across one of the world's most visually dramatic regions. From the fog-draped towers of the Golden Gate to the golden hills of the East Bay at dusk, every frame tells a story of place.

My work spans aerial and ground-level perspectives, urban and wild, still and in motion. I believe the Bay Area is a subject that rewards obsession — no two mornings on the bridge are ever the same.

Available for editorial assignments, licensing, fine art prints, and commercial work. Inquiries welcome.
